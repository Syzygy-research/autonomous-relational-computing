Insurance and risk path

Can autonomous consequential exposure become a measurable risk object?

If consequential exposure can be represented, bounded computationally before execution, and evidenced afterward, insurers and reinsurers can examine whether that creates an underwritable object. This page does not answer the underwriting question for them.

Open risk lensInspect evidenceView architecture

What to inspect

Identity, authority, proposed consequence, reachability, constraints, exposure, settlement, execution, effect, UNKNOWN, evidence, receipts, history, revocation and expiry.

What not to infer

Synthetic evaluator results are not premium effects, loss reductions, capital relief, certification, or an insurability finding.
