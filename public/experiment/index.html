E07 · Dependency constrained release

Without CR

With CR

Computational result
Reachable exposure reduced under cap

Insurance interpretation: Insurance meaning depends on cap validity and dependency model quality.

E08 · Multidimensional exposure

One action touches amount, data scope, destination, and time.

Without CR

Single scalar permission misses material exposure dimensions.

With CR

CR binds multiple dimensions and invalidates material mutation.

Computational result
Mutation and reachability narrowed

Insurance interpretation: May inform policy wording, exclusions, and claims evidence if validated.

Why E06 matters

Representation is not risk reduction.

E06 intentionally shows that identifying a shared dependency does not itself reduce exposure. An enforceable consequential constraint is required to alter reachability. That control case makes the experiment harder to overstate and easier to falsify.
