Research Invitation

These are the proposed Consequential Reachability semantics, invariants, falsifiers, and canonical challenge cases. We are seeking independent criticism of the benchmark itself before building the full execution harness.

We are not asking researchers to validate VALIS.

  • What assumptions are hidden?
  • Which cases fail to discriminate?
  • Can Consequential Reachability be reduced naturally to existing abstractions?
  • What counterexample breaks an invariant?
  • What concurrency or distributed-state schedules are absent?
  • Are UNKNOWN and CONTESTED adequately distinguished?
  • Can a conventional composition preserve the same properties with comparable proof burden?
